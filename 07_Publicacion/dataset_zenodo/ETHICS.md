# Ethics statement — SICST reproducible package

## Legal framework

This project complies with Ecuador's Ley Orgánica de Protección de Datos
Personales (LOPDP, Registro Oficial Suplemento 459, 26 May 2021). All
data collection, storage, and publication decisions described here follow
the principles of data minimization, purpose limitation, and
pseudonymization established by that law.

## Informed consent

All field data (interviews, questionnaire responses) underlying both the
general project dataset (`07_Datos/`) and the Enfoque 1 comparative
experiment (`06_Experimento/`) were collected under the project's
approved informed-consent process, documented in `08_Etica/` of the main
repository. Participants were informed of the academic purpose of data
collection, their right to withdraw, and the intended use of anonymized
data in academic publications and open data deposits.

No consent authorizes the publication of identifiable data. Signatures,
national ID numbers, faces, and voices remain exclusively in the
restricted, encrypted evidence container
(`02_Evidencias/00_Restringido/`) and are never included in this public
package.

## Use of a Large Language Model as part of the study

The Enfoque 1 experiment compares functional requirements elicited by the
human team against functional requirements generated by an LLM
(GPT-5.5-mini) from the same anonymized interview transcripts. This use
of an LLM is the object of study itself, not merely a writing aid, and is
fully documented — including the exact prompts, the model's
self-reported version, and its explicit confirmation that no information
external to the supplied transcripts was used — in
`06_Experimento/prompts_llm/prompt_generacion_RF_llm.md`. No participant
data was shared with the LLM beyond the same anonymized transcripts
already used for human elicitation.

## Blind evaluation

The four independent judges who rated the 66 anonymized functional
requirements were informed that they were evaluating a set of
requirements of undisclosed origin, and consented informally as
volunteer academic collaborators. Their identities are limited to first
names in the raw data, at their own request, and no other personal data
about them is collected or published.

## Preregistration and research integrity

The comparative experiment's protocol, hypotheses, and analysis plan were
preregistered on the Open Science Framework (OSF) prior to generating the
LLM-produced requirements or collecting the blind evaluations (DOI
10.17605/OSF.IO/82Q76, registered 2026-09-12). This preregistration
protects against p-hacking and HARKing (hypothesizing after results are
known); any deviation from the registered plan is documented explicitly
in `06_Experimento/osf_deviations.pdf`.
